# Working with Larkspur's people

Larkspur's stakeholders are played by Claude from a sealed file you don't read. They know things you don't, they answer what you ask rather than what would help, and they remember every conversation.

## How they reach you

Issues labeled client are messages from Larkspur. Each one opens with who it's from: Dana Whitfield, Marcus Oyelaran, Priya Raman, Tom Kessler or Jo Alvarez. Client events arrive Monday mornings. Reply in the issue the way you'd reply to a client email, and the person answers the next morning, about 7am Eastern.

## How you reach them

* Email: comment on any client issue, or open a new issue labeled client addressed to someone by name
* Meetings: in a Claude chat, ask to meet someone, for example "meet Priya about read access to FREIGHTCORE". Prepare the way you would for a real meeting; they hold back what you don't ask about
* Deliverables: open a pull request with the document under docs/deliverables/ and label it deliverable. The stakeholder it's for replies in character the next morning, followed by a grader's scores against a rubric you haven't seen

## What's real here

The data, the systems and their failures behave the same whether or not you're looking. What the stakeholders say is consistent with the data, so when a number they give you disagrees with what you find, one of you is wrong, and finding out which is part of the work.
